Job Scanner + Bider settings

Search Chrome profile: Enable scan ON, Enable apply OFF. Stay logged in on CrowdWorks / Lancers / Coconala. The extension fetches listing URLs with this profile’s cookies and POSTs jobs to Railway. Railway never stores platform passwords.

Apply Chrome profile: Enable scan OFF, Enable apply ON. Opens queued jobs from the shared Railway queue. Does not scan. Never auto-submits.

Alarm interval: Dashboard source intervals are honored as much as Manifest V3 allows. Chrome alarms usually fire at most every 30 seconds (unpacked) or 1 minute (packed). A 20s dashboard interval therefore becomes ~30s–1min, not 20s. Per-source last-run in extension storage still skips a source until its interval has elapsed.
